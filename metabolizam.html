Metabolizam rakaCancer metabolism

Rak kao metabolička bolestCancer as a metabolic disease

Prof. Thomas Seyfriedi Dr Roman o ishrani na terapijiand Dr Roman on eating during treatment

Prof. Thomas Seyfried, biolog sa Boston College-a, nastavlja rad nemačkog naučnika Ota Varburga iz 1920-ih do 1940-ih. Po njemu rak ne počinje u genima, nego u mitohondrijama, malim elektranama u svakoj ćeliji. Iz toga izvodi i drugačiji pristup lečenju.Prof. Thomas Seyfried, a biologist at Boston College, continues the work of German scientist Otto Warburg from the 1920s to 1940s. In his view cancer does not start in the genes but in the mitochondria, the tiny power plants in every cell. From that he derives a different approach to treatment.

Važno: ovo je pogled kojem se Seyfried posvetio celu karijeru, ali on sam kaže da ga glavna struja onkologije još ne prihvata. Prikazujemo ga onako kako ga on objašnjava, a na kraju stranice i gde se razlikuje od saveta Dr Romana.Important: this is the view Seyfried has devoted his career to, but he says himself that mainstream oncology does not yet accept it. We present it as he explains it, and at the end of the page where it differs from Dr Roman's advice.

MitohondrijeMitochondria

Elektrana koja određuje sudbinu ćelijeThe power plant that decides a cell's fate

Mitohondrije su mreže cevčica u svakoj ćeliji. Sve mitohondrije nasleđujemo od majke, u trenutku začeća. Kada dišemo, one koriste kiseonik da od hrane naprave ATP, energetsku valutu ćelije. Otpadni proizvodi su samo ugljen-dioksid i voda.Mitochondria are networks of tiny tubes in every cell. We inherit all of them from our mother, at conception. As we breathe, they use oxygen to turn food into ATP, the cell's energy currency. The only waste is carbon dioxide and water.

Seyfried kaže da mitohondrije rade i više od toga: govore ćeliji kada da se deli, a kada da stane, i razgovaraju sa susednim ćelijama u jetri, plućima, crevima ili mozgu. Njihovo trošenje tokom života je deo starenja.Seyfried says mitochondria do more than that: they tell the cell when to divide and when to stop, and they talk to neighbouring cells in the liver, lungs, gut or brain. Their wear and tear over a lifetime is part of ageing.

Kako nastaje rak, po SeyfrieduHow cancer starts, according to Seyfried

Od oštećenja do nekontrolisanog rastaFrom damage to uncontrolled growth

  1. OštećenjeDamage

    Kancerogeni, prekidi disanja u snu (apneja), virusi, hronična upala, mikroplastika, „večne hemikalije“, pesticidi i retke nasledne mutacije stvaraju reaktivne čestice koje oštećuju mitohondrije.Carcinogens, breathing pauses in sleep (apnoea), viruses, chronic inflammation, microplastics, "forever chemicals", pesticides and rare inherited mutations create reactive particles that damage mitochondria.

  2. Brzo ili sporoFast or slow

    Ako je oštećenje naglo (kao kod cijanida), ćelija umire. Ako je sporo i hronično, ćelija preživi, ali gubi efikasnu energiju.If the damage is sudden (as with cyanide), the cell dies. If it is slow and chronic, the cell survives but loses efficient energy.

  3. Povratak drevnom vrenjuBack to ancient fermentation

    Ćelija se vraća načinu dobijanja energije iz vremena pre kiseonika na Zemlji, kada su ćelije rasle bez kontrole. Hrani se glukozom i glutaminom i izbacuje mlečnu i ćilibarnu kiselinu.The cell falls back on how energy was made before there was oxygen on Earth, when cells grew without control. It feeds on glucose and glutamine and releases lactic and succinic acid.

  4. Gladna ćelijaA hungry cell

    Vrenje daje 2–4 ATP umesto 34–36, pa ćelija mora da uvlači ogromne količine goriva. Mitohondrija šalje signal jedru da otvori „vrata“ za glukozu i glutamin. To je nekontrolisan rast koji zovemo rak.Fermentation gives 2–4 ATP instead of 34–36, so the cell must pull in huge amounts of fuel. The mitochondrion signals the nucleus to open the "gates" for glucose and glutamine. That is the uncontrolled growth we call cancer.

Ogled sa jedromThe nucleus experiment

Seyfried navodi oglede u kojima je jedro tumorske ćelije stavljeno u zdravu ćeliju bez jedra, i ćelija je ostala zdrava. Kada je zdravo jedro stavljeno u citoplazmu tumorske ćelije, rast je bio nekontrolisan. Za njega to znači da ključ nije u jedru, nego u mitohondrijama.Seyfried cites experiments where a tumour cell's nucleus was placed into a healthy cell without a nucleus, and the cell stayed healthy. When a healthy nucleus was placed into a tumour cell's cytoplasm, growth was uncontrolled. To him this means the key lies not in the nucleus but in the mitochondria.

Nasledne mutacijeInherited mutations

Mutacije kao BRCA1 povećavaju rizik, ali nijedna ne dovodi do raka kod 100% nosilaca. Njegova grupa je pokazala da svaka od njih na neki način remeti rad mitohondrija.Mutations like BRCA1 raise risk, but none leads to cancer in 100% of carriers. His group showed that each of them disturbs mitochondrial function in some way.

Vuk i pasThe wolf and the dog

Rak je glavni uzrok smrti domaćih pasa, a vukovi u divljini ga retko imaju. Vuk trči i jede prirodnu hranu; pas živi u stanu, šeta jednom dnevno i postaje gojazan. Seyfried tako objašnjava zašto bogate zemlje imaju više raka: prerađeni ugljeni hidrati, sedenje, stres i loš san.Cancer is the top killer of pet dogs, while wild wolves rarely get it. The wolf runs and eats natural food; the dog lives in a flat, gets walked once a day and becomes obese. Seyfried uses this to explain why rich countries have more cancer: processed carbohydrates, inactivity, stress and poor sleep.

GKI indeksThe GKI index

Jedan broj za zdravlje mitohondrijaOne number for mitochondrial health

Seyfriedova grupa je razvila indeks glukoze i ketona (GKI). Nastao je zahvaljujući jednoj pacijentkinji, Trudi Dupont, koja je merila šećer i ketone posebno i uplašila se kada joj je šećer skočio posle svađe oko parkinga. Ketoni su ostali isti. Odnos ta dva broja pokazao se mnogo stabilnijim od svakog pojedinačno.Seyfried's group developed the glucose ketone index (GKI). It came about thanks to one patient, Trudy Dupont, who measured sugar and ketones separately and got scared when her sugar jumped after an argument over a parking space. Her ketones had not changed. The ratio of the two proved far more stable than either alone.

Meri se iz kapi krvi iz prsta, aparatom za glukozu i ketone. GKI = glukoza (mmol/L) ÷ ketoni (mmol/L). Ako aparat daje glukozu u mg/dL, podelite je sa 18.It is measured from a finger-prick drop of blood with a glucose and ketone meter. GKI = glucose (mmol/L) ÷ ketones (mmol/L). If your meter shows glucose in mg/dL, divide it by 18.

12,5

Primer iz razgovora: glukoza 90 mg/dL (5 mmol/L) i ketoni 0,4 daju GKI 12,5. Seyfried je za taj broj rekao da je u „zoni prevencije“.Example from the interview: glucose 90 mg/dL (5 mmol/L) and ketones 0.4 give a GKI of 12.5. Seyfried said that number is in the "prevention zone".

Crvena zona: rizikRed zone: risk

Mnogo prerađenih ugljenih hidrata, bez kretanja, česti obroci. Šećer visok, ketona gotovo nema. Kod nekih ljudi GKI dostiže i 500. „Možemo da posetimo crvenu zonu, ali ne želimo da živimo u njoj.“Lots of processed carbs, no exercise, frequent meals. Sugar high, almost no ketones. In some people GKI reaches 500. "We can visit the red zone, we don't want to live in it."

Zona prevencijePrevention zone

Ovde je, kaže Seyfried, uglavnom živeo paleolitski čovek: manje šećera, više kretanja, periodi bez hrane. U ovoj zoni je teško dobiti rak i hronične bolesti.This, Seyfried says, is where Palaeolithic humans mostly lived: less sugar, more movement, periods without food. In this zone it is hard to get cancer and chronic diseases.

Zelena zona: lečenjeGreen zone: management

Nizak šećer i viši ketoni. Za osobe koje već imaju rak, cilj je da što duže budu ovde, uz nadzor lekara, jer se tako tumoru oduzima jedno od dva goriva.Low sugar and higher ketones. For people who already have cancer, the aim is to stay here as long as possible, under medical supervision, because it takes away one of the tumour's two fuels.

Seyfried je pokazao grafikon zona u razgovoru, ali ga ovde ne prenosimo brojčano. Granice zona proverite u njegovom radu (vidi Izvore) ili sa lekarom.Seyfried showed a chart of the zones in the interview, but we do not reproduce its numbers here. Check the zone limits in his paper (see Sources) or with your doctor.

Metabolička terapijaMetabolic therapy

„Press-pulse“: pritisak i puls"Press-pulse": press and pulse

Seyfried ne odbacuje hemioterapiju i zračenje. Kaže da ih treba koristiti drugačije: u manjim dozama, kada je telo u stanju nutritivne ketoze.Seyfried does not reject chemotherapy and radiation. He says they should be used differently: at lower doses, while the body is in nutritional ketosis.

  1. Pritisak (press)Press

    Ishranom spustiti glukozu i podići ketone (zelena zona). Zdrave ćelije mogu da koriste ketone, a tumorske, po Seyfriedu, ne mogu jer su im mitohondrije oštećene.Lower glucose and raise ketones with diet (green zone). Healthy cells can use ketones, while tumour cells, according to Seyfried, cannot because their mitochondria are damaged.

  2. Puls (pulse)Pulse

    Lekovima gađati drugo gorivo, glutamin. Njegova laboratorija je ispitala mebendazol. Kaže da tumor tada postaje mnogo osetljiviji na niske doze hemioterapije, zračenja ili imunoterapije.Use drugs to target the other fuel, glutamine. His lab tested mebendazole. He says the tumour then becomes much more sensitive to low doses of chemotherapy, radiation or immunotherapy.

  3. Manje dozeLower doses

    Prema Seyfriedu, klinike sa kojima sarađuje u Istanbulu i Grčkoj kombinuju ketozu sa standardnim lekovima u znatno nižim dozama, i kod raka pankreasa i uznapredovalog raka dojke pacijenti žive i po četiri do pet godina.According to Seyfried, the clinics he works with in Istanbul and Greece combine ketosis with standard drugs at much lower doses, and patients with pancreatic and advanced breast cancer live four to five years.

Primeri koje navodiExamples he cites

  • Pablo Kelly iz Devona (Engleska) imao je glioblastom za koji je rečeno da se ne može operisati. Odbio je zračenje i hemioterapiju i koristio samo metaboličku terapiju. Živeo je 10 godina; tumor je postao operabilan i operisan je četiri puta. Preminuo je od krvarenja posle poslednje operacije, ne od tumora.Pablo Kelly from Devon (England) had a glioblastoma said to be inoperable. He declined radiation and chemotherapy and used only metabolic therapy. He lived 10 years; the tumour became operable and was operated on four times. He died of a bleed after the last surgery, not of the tumour.
  • Grupa u Grčkoj kod glioblastoma koristi Mediteransku ishranu sa manje kalorija (losos, sardine, maslinovo ulje, avokado) i vežbanje.A group in Greece uses a lower-calorie Mediterranean diet (salmon, sardines, olive oil, avocado) and exercise for glioblastoma.
  • Studija na miševima iz 2013. (sa Dominikom D'Agostinom): keto ishrana sama usporava tumor, a uz hiperbaričnu komoru kiseonika efekat je mnogo jači.A 2013 mouse study (with Dominic D'Agostino): a keto diet alone slows the tumour, and with hyperbaric oxygen the effect is much stronger.

MetastazeMetastasis

Seyfried kaže da tumor ubija kada se proširi. Ćelije koje se šire po telu, po njemu, nastaju spajanjem tumorske ćelije i ćelije imunskog sistema (makrofaga). Hrane se glutaminom, pa su osetljive na metaboličku terapiju, uz malo imunoterapije na kraju.Seyfried says tumours kill when they spread. The cells that travel through the body, in his view, arise from a fusion of a tumour cell and an immune cell (macrophage). They run on glutamine, so they are sensitive to metabolic therapy, with a little immunotherapy at the end.

Seyfried nikada ne koristi reč „izlečenje“. Govori o tome da se bolest drži pod kontrolom i da ljudi žive duže i bolje.Seyfried never uses the word "cure". He talks about keeping the disease under control so people live longer and better.

Post i oprezFasting and caution

Ketoza nije za svakoga bez nadzoraKetosis is not for everyone without supervision

„Zid“ trećeg danaThe day-three "wall"

Posle oko tri dana samo vode mnogi udare u „zid“: loš san, nemirne noge, loše osećanje. Seyfried zato pacijente prvo nedelju dana drži na ishrani bez ugljenih hidrata, pa tek onda prelaze na post uz vodu. Glukozu poredi sa kokainom po tome koliko mozak zavisi od nje.After about three days of water only, many hit a "wall": poor sleep, restless legs, feeling awful. So Seyfried first keeps patients on a zero-carb diet for a week, and only then do they move to water fasting. He compares glucose to cocaine in how hooked the brain is on it.

Ko mora da paziWho must be careful

Ljudi sa dijabetesom, visokim pritiskom i drugim bolestima, i oni sa manjkom karnitina (koji sprečava pretvaranje masti u ketone). Takvoj osobi lekar mora da prilagodi terapiju. Bilo je slučajeva da je osoba sa drugim bolestima izgubila svest tokom ketoze.People with diabetes, high blood pressure and other conditions, and those with carnitine deficiency (which stops fat being turned into ketones). For them a doctor must adjust treatment. There have been cases of a person with other conditions collapsing during ketosis.

Gubitak težineWeight loss

Onkolozi se boje kaheksije, opasnog gubitka mišića kod raka. Seyfried pravi razliku: kaheksija je kada tumor „topi“ mišiće da bi uzeo glutamin, a gubitak masti u ketozi je, kako kaže, terapijski.Oncologists fear cachexia, the dangerous muscle wasting of cancer. Seyfried draws a line: cachexia is when the tumour "melts" muscle to take glutamine, while losing fat in ketosis is, he says, therapeutic.

Gde se razlikujuWhere they differ

Dva pogleda na ishranu tokom terapijeTwo views on eating during treatment

Ovde se dva stručnjaka čije razgovore smo slušali ne slažu. Oba pogleda prenosimo verno, da biste ih mogli poneti svom lekaru.Here two of the experts we listened to disagree. We report both views faithfully, so you can take them to your doctor.

Prof. Seyfried

Standardni saveti da se tokom terapije „jede šta god može“ pune krv šećerom i hrane tumor. Ketoza uz nadzor lekara čuva zdrave ćelije, slabi tumor i omogućava manje doze lekova.Standard advice to "eat whatever you can" during treatment floods the blood with sugar and feeds the tumour. Ketosis under medical supervision protects healthy cells, weakens the tumour and allows lower drug doses.

Dr Roman

Najčešća greška koju viđa je stroga dijeta ili post tokom terapije. Pacijenti izgube težinu, oslabe, slabije zarastaju i lečenje mora da se prekine ili promeni. Tokom terapije potreba za proteinima raste, i nijedna ishrana nije najbolja za sve.The most common mistake he sees is a strict diet or fasting during treatment. Patients lose weight, grow weak, heal poorly, and treatment has to be paused or changed. During treatment protein needs go up, and no single diet is best for everyone.

Zajedničko im je: bez prerađene hrane, šećernih pića i alkohola, uz kretanje, dobar san i manje stresa. Ako razmišljate o ketozi ili postu, uradite to samo uz svog onkologa i nutricionistu koji poznaje metaboličku terapiju.What they share: no processed food, sugary drinks or alcohol, plus movement, good sleep and less stress. If you are considering ketosis or fasting, do it only with your oncologist and a dietitian who knows metabolic therapy.

PrevencijaPrevention

Šta bi Seyfried uradio „kao kralj“What Seyfried would do "as king"

Na pitanje šta bi uradio da može da odlučuje, Seyfried je nabrojao ovo. Dodao je da nijedna vlast ne treba da ljudima naređuje šta da jedu; ljudi treba da imaju znanje i sami biraju.Asked what he would do if he could decide, Seyfried listed these. He added that no government should tell people what to eat; people should have the knowledge and choose for themselves.

  • Obrazovanje: ljudi treba da znaju šta čuva mitohondrije.Education: people should know what protects mitochondria.
  • Zdrava hrana dostupna i siromašnima, ne samo skupi biftek u specijalizovanim radnjama.Healthy food affordable for the poor, not just expensive steaks in specialty shops.
  • Izbegavati ultra-prerađenu hranu i visokofruktozni kukuruzni sirup.Avoid ultra-processed food and high-fructose corn syrup.
  • Kretanje i vežbanje.Movement and exercise.
  • Manje stresa: muzika, meditacija, prijatelji, sreća.Less stress: music, meditation, friends, happiness.
  • Dobar san, kada se mitohondrije odmaraju.Good sleep, when mitochondria rest.
  • Zabraniti „večne hemikalije“ i očistiti vodu od teških metala.Ban "forever chemicals" and clean heavy metals out of the water.

Jedan japanski kolega mu je to rekao obrnuto: ako želite rak, jedite smeće, spavajte loše, ne silazite sa kauča, skrolujte ceo dan, ne vežbajte i nemojte imati prijatelje.A Japanese colleague put it the other way round: if you want cancer, eat junk, sleep badly, never leave the couch, scroll all day, don't exercise and have no friends.
