Prenosimo ih svojim rečima, ne doslovno, osim kratkih citata.The content on drugs, metabolism, food, treatment and reports reports what doctors and scientists said in public talks and interviews. Each claim on the site names who made it. We retell them in our own words, not verbatim, apart from short quotes.

Kako koristiti ove informacijeHow to use this information

Ovi stručnjaci se ne slažu uvek, a neka tvrđenja (na primer metabolička teorija raka) glavna struja medicine još ne prihvata. Iskoristite ovaj sajt da bolje razumete svoju situaciju i postavite prava pitanja. Odluke o lečenju donosite sa svojim lekarima.These experts don't always agree, and some claims (for example the metabolic theory of cancer) are not yet accepted by mainstream medicine. Use this site to understand your situation better and ask the right questions. Make treatment decisions with your doctors.
