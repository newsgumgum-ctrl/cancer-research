Antiparazitici i rakAntiparasitics and cancer

Fenbendazol, ivermektin i mebendazol: šta zaista znamoFenbendazole, ivermectin and mebendazole: what we really know

Dr RomanProf. Thomas SeyfriedPacijenti Mike i RyanPatients Mike and Ryan

O ova tri leka se mnogo priča na društvenim mrežama. Dr Roman, hematolog-onkolog, pregledao je sve objavljene podatke i pozvao pacijente koji ih uzimaju da ispričaju svoju priču. Na ovoj stranici prenosimo šta je pronašao, šta su pacijenti doživeli i kako ove lekove vidi prof. Seyfried.These three drugs are talked about a lot on social media. Dr Roman, a haematologist-oncologist, reviewed all the published data and invited patients who take them to tell their story. This page reports what he found, what the patients experienced, and how Prof. Seyfried sees these drugs.

„Ne kažem vam da ga uzimate i ne kažem vam da ga ne uzimate. Samo vam donosim činjenice.“"I'm not telling you to take it and I'm not telling you not to take it. I'm just bringing you the facts."

Dr Roman

OsnoveThe basics

Tri leka protiv parazitaThree antiparasitic drugs

Nijedan od njih nije odobren za lečenje raka.None of them is approved to treat cancer.

Za ljudeFor people

MebendazolMebendazole

Lek protiv crevnih parazita. Ometa stvaranje tubulina u parazitima i tako ih ubija. Ljudi ga obično uzimaju kratko.A drug against intestinal parasites. It interferes with tubulin formation in parasites and so kills them. People usually take it for a short time.

Za ljudeFor people

IvermektinIvermectin

Lek protiv parazita koji se koristi kod ljudi. Ryan ga je za svoju upotrebu donosio iz Meksika.An antiparasitic drug used in people. Ryan brought his supply back from Mexico.

Za životinjeFor animals

FenbendazolFenbendazole

Veterinarski lek. Ryan kaže da su mu lekari rekli da „nije za ljudsku upotrebu“. Prodaja preko Amazona je prekinuta posle istrage BBC-ja u Velsu.A veterinary drug. Ryan says his doctors told him it is "not for human consumption". Sales on Amazon stopped after a BBC investigation in Wales.

LaboratorijaThe laboratory

U laboratoriji mebendazol izgleda obećavajućeIn the lab, mebendazole looks promising

Dr Roman navodi najmanje 17 pretkliničkih studija u poslednje dve decenije, na ćelijama, miševima i tumorskim ćelijama uzetim od pacijenata. Najviše su ispitivani tumori mozga (glioblastom, meduloblastom), ali i rak debelog creva, dojke, pluća, želuca, leukemije, mijelom, limfom, rak jajnika, pankreasa i bubrega.Dr Roman cites at least 17 preclinical studies over two decades, on cells, mice and tumour cells taken from patients. Brain tumours (glioblastoma, medulloblastoma) were studied most, along with bowel, breast, lung and stomach cancer, leukaemias, myeloma, lymphoma, and ovarian, pancreatic and kidney cancer.

Kako bi mogao da delujeHow it might work

  • Ometa tubulin, slično taksanima i vinka alkaloidima, lekovima koji se danas koriste u hemioterapijiBlocks tubulin, like the taxanes and vinca alkaloids used in chemotherapy today
  • Sprečava rast novih krvnih sudova koji hrane tumorPrevents new blood vessels that feed the tumour
  • Pokreće prirodnu smrt oštećenih ćelija (apoptozu)Triggers the natural death of damaged cells (apoptosis)
  • Koči signalne puteve rasta raka (JAK-STAT, MEK, hedgehog)Slows cancer growth pathways (JAK-STAT, MEK, hedgehog)
  • Podstiče imunski odgovor i smanjuje matične ćelije rakaBoosts the immune response and reduces cancer stem cells
  • Remeti metabolizam tumoraDisrupts tumour metabolism

Šta su studije pokazaleWhat the studies showed

  • Ubija ćelije mnogih vrsta raka, a uglavnom štedi zdrave ćelijeKills cells of many cancer types while largely sparing healthy cells
  • Koči rast krvnih sudova tumoraSlows tumour blood vessel growth
  • Pomaže u sprečavanju širenja (metastaza)Helps prevent spread (metastasis)
  • Pojačava dejstvo hemioterapije: temozolomida, irinotekana i gefitinibaEnhances chemotherapy: temozolomide, irinotecan and gefitinib

Zašto laboratorija nije dovoljnaWhy the lab is not enough

Dr Roman objašnjava da oko 95% lekova koji u laboratoriji deluju na rak ne uspe kada se ispita na ljudima. Primer su inhibitori receptora IGF-1: u laboratoriji su izgledali kao veliki uspeh, kompanije su u kliničke studije uložile oko 1,6 milijardi dolara, a u studijama faze 2 i 3 nisu uspeli. Zato su studije na ljudima neophodne.Dr Roman explains that about 95% of drugs that work against cancer in the lab fail when tested in people. One example is the IGF-1 receptor inhibitors: they looked like a home run in the lab, companies spent about 1.6 billion dollars on clinical trials, and they failed in phase 2 and 3. That is why human studies are essential.

Zaključak Dr RomanaDr Roman's conclusion

  • Pretklinički dokazi su jaki.The preclinical evidence is strong.
  • U uobičajenim dozama mebendazol je bezbedan, ali nije bez neželjenih efekata.At standard doses mebendazole is safe, but not free of side effects.
  • Dokazi kod ljudi su ograničeni i nedovoljni. Jedina pozitivna randomizovana studija je mala studija raka debelog creva.Human evidence is limited and inconclusive. The only positive randomised study is one small bowel cancer trial.
  • Nijedna velika smernica ga ne preporučuje ni za jednu vrstu raka. Potrebne su velike randomizovane studije.No major guideline recommends it for any cancer. Large randomised trials are needed.
  • Doza nije poznata. Doze koje neki lekari preporučuju zasnovane su na pričama pacijenata, a ne na studijama.The dose is unknown. Doses some doctors recommend are based on patient stories, not on studies.
BezbednostSafety

Veoma bezbedan, ali ne „kao prečišćena voda“Very safe, but not "like purified water"

Bezbednost mebendazola ispitana je kod 6.276 osoba u 39 studija. Dr Roman upozorava na tvrdnje sa društvenih mreža da su ovi lekovi bezbedni kao voda: neželjeni efekti postoje i opisani su.Mebendazole's safety was studied in 6,276 people across 39 trials. Dr Roman warns against social-media claims that these drugs are as safe as water: side effects exist and have been reported.

ČestiCommon

Gubitak apetita, bol u stomaku, mučnina, povraćanje, proliv, gasovi, osip.Loss of appetite, stomach pain, nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea, gas, rash.

Retki, ali ozbiljniRare but serious

  • Pad belih krvnih zrnaca (agranulocitoza), češće uz veće doze ili duže uzimanje, uz rizik od teških infekcijaVery low white cell counts (agranulocytosis), more often with higher doses or long use, with a risk of severe infection
  • Teške kožne reakcije (Stevens-Johnson, TEN), naročito uz metronidazol, pa tu kombinaciju treba izbegavatiSevere skin reactions (Stevens-Johnson, TEN), especially with metronidazole, so avoid that combination
  • Napadi (veoma retko), upala jetre, upala bubrega, alergijska reakcija, opadanje koseSeizures (very rare), hepatitis, kidney inflammation, allergic reaction, hair loss

Problem upijanjaThe absorption problem

Mebendazol se slabo i neujednačeno upija iz creva, a postoji više formulacija. Zato je teško postići nivo u krvi potreban za dejstvo na tumor. U studiji raka digestivnog trakta samo polovina pacijenata je dostigla ciljni nivo. Ispituju se nove formulacije (polimorf C).Mebendazole is poorly and unevenly absorbed from the gut, and several formulations exist. That makes it hard to reach the blood level needed to act on a tumour. In the gastrointestinal cancer study only half of patients reached the target level. New formulations (polymorph C) are being studied.

Priče pacijenataPatient stories

Dva pacijenta koja su se javilaTwo patients who came forward

Dr Roman je dugo pozivao ljude koji tvrde da su im ovi lekovi pomogli da ispričaju svoju priču. Javila su se dvojica. Obojica kažu da veruju da im lekovi pomažu, i obojica su iskreno rekli šta ne znaju.For a long time Dr Roman invited people who say these drugs helped them to share their story. Two came forward. Both say they believe the drugs help them, and both were honest about what they don't know.

Rak debelog creva, stadijum 4Bowel cancer, stage 4

Mike

  1. 2022

    Hitna operacija, uklonjeno oko 30 cm creva. Tumor na levoj strani, bez mutacija RAS i BRAF. Hemioterapija FOLFOX, pa Vectibix.Emergency surgery, about 30 cm of bowel removed. Left-sided tumour without RAS or BRAF mutations. FOLFOX chemotherapy, then Vectibix.

  2. 2023

    Krvni test na tumorsku DNK (Signatera) negativan, terapija prekinuta. Bez lečenja oko dve i po godine.Tumour DNA blood test (Signatera) negative, treatment stopped. About two and a half years without treatment.

  3. 2025

    Test ponovo pozitivan, vrednosti rastu. U decembru masa kod rektuma i teški simptomi: ne može da spava, stalni nagon za toaletom.The test turns positive again and keeps rising. In December a mass near the rectum and severe symptoms: no sleep, constant urge to go to the toilet.

  4. jan 2026Jan 2026

    Počinje fenbendazol (5 dana da, 2 ne) i ivermektin svaki dan. Za oko 9 dana gotovo svi simptomi nestaju, pre ponovnog početka hemioterapije.Starts fenbendazole (5 days on, 2 off) and ivermectin daily. Within about 9 days almost all symptoms ease, before chemotherapy restarts.

  5. maj 2026May 2026

    Hemioterapija (5-FU, Vectibix do aprila) uz oba leka. PET: limfni čvor nestao, aktivnost pala sa 3,4 na 2,1. Hemioterapija prekinuta.Chemotherapy (5-FU, Vectibix until April) alongside both drugs. PET: lymph node gone, uptake down from 3.4 to 2.1. Chemotherapy stopped.

  6. avg 2026Aug 2026

    Samo fenbendazol i ivermektin. PET stabilan, blago smanjenje. Oseća se odlično, vežba i vesla.Only fenbendazole and ivermectin. PET stable, slight shrinkage. Feels great, lifts and rows.

„Verujem da ovi lekovi bar delimično deluju, jer je bilo i hemioterapije. Može li se to dokazati? Ne vidim kako, osim po onome što sam osetio.“"I believe these drugs at least partially work, because there was chemo involved. Can you prove that? I don't see how, other than what I felt."

Mike

Šta Dr Roman primećujeWhat Dr Roman notes

  • Hemioterapija i lekovi su uzimani zajedno, pa se ne može znati šta je delovalo.Chemotherapy and the drugs were taken together, so we cannot know what worked.
  • Tumori na levoj strani i bez RAS/BRAF mutacija imaju bolju prognozu.Left-sided tumours without RAS/BRAF mutations have a better outlook.
  • Jak osip od Vectibixa često znači da tumor dobro odgovara na taj lek.A strong rash from Vectibix often means the tumour responds well to that drug.
  • Poboljšanje za 9 dana pre hemioterapije ne može da objasni. Placebo je moguć, ali ga ne odbacuje olako: „Može biti placebo, ali može biti i stvarno.“He cannot explain the improvement in 9 days before chemotherapy. Placebo is possible, but he does not brush it off: "It could be placebo, but it could be real."
Rak mokraćne bešikeBladder cancer

Ryan, 42

  1. 2019

    Krv u mokraći. Uklonjen bubreg zbog karcinoma prelaznog epitela, samo operacija.Blood in the urine. Kidney removed for transitional cell carcinoma, surgery only.

  2. 2021–23

    Tumori u bešici, uključujući ravni oblik (CIS), bez prodora u mišić. Jedna kura mitomicina. Lekari predlažu BCG ili uklanjanje bešike; on to odbija.Tumours in the bladder, including the flat form (CIS), not invading the muscle. One course of mitomycin. Doctors suggest BCG or bladder removal; he declines.

  3. jul 2025Jul 2025

    Počinje fenbendazol 444 mg dnevno i ivermektin 12 mg nedeljno (ivermektin sa pauzom od decembra do avgusta).Starts fenbendazole 444 mg daily and ivermectin 12 mg weekly (with a break in ivermectin from December to August).

  4. feb 2026Feb 2026

    Cistoskopija: tumor od oko 2 cm i ravna promena od oko 5 × 5 cm. Posle toga odbija cistoskopije i prati se samo MR-om.Cystoscopy: a tumour of about 2 cm and a flat area of about 5 × 5 cm. After that he declines cystoscopies and is followed only by MRI.

  5. danasnow

    Oseća se odlično. Lekar mu je rekao da se njegova bolest „ponaša neobično“ i da bi širenje moglo da potraje i do dve godine.Feels great. His doctor told him his disease is "behaving unusually" and that spread could take up to two years.

„Ne mogu da kažem da je lek. Osećam se odlično, ljudi kažu da izgledam odlično. Mislim da ovo treba istražiti.“"I can't say it's a cure. I feel great, people say I look great. I feel like it needs to be looked into."

Ryan

Šta Dr Roman primećujeWhat Dr Roman notes

  • Tumor je bio prisutan i posle sedam meseci uzimanja lekova, ali Ryan veruje da bi bez njih bilo gore.The tumour was still present after seven months on the drugs, but Ryan believes it would be worse without them.
  • Mali tumori bešike ne vide se na MR-u. Samo cistoskopija ih otkriva pre nego što prodru u mišić, kada lečenje postaje mnogo teže.Small bladder tumours do not show on MRI. Only cystoscopy finds them before they invade the muscle, when treatment becomes much harder.
  • Zato ga moli da ponovo razmisli o cistoskopiji: „Bolje sprečiti nego lečiti.“So he asks him to reconsider cystoscopy: "Better safe than sorry."

Druga strana pričaThe other side of stories

Dr Roman podseća da priče idu u oba smera. Jedan komentator je napisao da je mebendazol 500 mg uzimao 10 meseci i da „ne deluje, nula“. Ako prihvatimo priče u kojima je pomoglo, fer je saslušati i one u kojima nije. U svojoj praksi viđa pacijente koji poboljšanje pripisuju ovim lekovima, a istovremeno primaju operaciju, hemioterapiju, zračenje ili imunoterapiju.Dr Roman points out that stories cut both ways. One commenter wrote that he took mebendazole 500 mg for 10 months and it was "not working, zero". If we accept the stories where it helped, it is fair to hear the ones where it didn't. In his practice he sees patients who credit these drugs for improvement while also receiving surgery, chemotherapy, radiation or immunotherapy.

Zato postavlja pitanje: ako su ovi lekovi jeftini, a neki lekari od konsultacija za njih dobro žive, zašto ti lekari ne sprovedu kliničku studiju? Za studiju su potrebni protokol, pacijenti i lek.So he asks: if these drugs are cheap and some doctors make a comfortable living from consultations about them, why don't those doctors run a clinical trial? A trial needs a protocol, patients and the drug.

Drugi pogledAnother view

Kako mebendazol vidi prof. SeyfriedHow Prof. Seyfried sees mebendazole

Prof. Seyfried kaže da tumor raste na dva goriva, glukozi i glutaminu. Njegova laboratorija je objavila rad u kome pokazuje da mebendazol cilja oba. On ga ne koristi samog, već kao „puls“ u strategiji „press-pulse“: ishranom se spušta glukoza, a lekovima se gađa glutamin, uz standardne terapije u nižim dozama.Prof. Seyfried says tumours grow on two fuels, glucose and glutamine. His lab published a paper showing that mebendazole targets both. He does not use it alone but as a "pulse" in his "press-pulse" strategy: diet lowers glucose, and drugs target glutamine, alongside standard therapies at lower doses.

Ako ipak odlučiteIf you decide to try

Kako to raditi što bezbednijeHow to do it as safely as possible

Dr Roman kaže: ako krenete u ovakav režim, neka vas prati zdravstveni radnik i neka se rade analize, da ne napravite više štete nego koristi.Dr Roman says: if you start such a regimen, be followed by a healthcare provider and have your labs monitored, so you do no more harm than good.

Pre početka i tokom uzimanjaBefore and during

  • Recite onkologu šta i u kojoj dozi uzimate.Tell your oncologist what you take and how much.
  • Ne preskačite snimanja, preglede ni standardno lečenje.Do not skip scans, check-ups or standard treatment.
  • Jetreni enzimi (AST, ALT, GGT, bilirubin) i krvna slika pre početka, pa svake 2–4 nedelje.Liver enzymes (AST, ALT, GGT, bilirubin) and blood count before starting, then every 2–4 weeks.
  • Mebendazol ne kombinovati sa metronidazolom.Do not combine mebendazole with metronidazole.
  • Bez alkohola i grejpfruta. Paracetamol samo povremeno.No alcohol or grapefruit. Paracetamol only now and then.
  • Ako dobijete novi lek (na primer antibiotik), recite lekaru šta uzimate.If you are prescribed a new drug (for example an antibiotic), tell the doctor what you take.

Analize uredneResults normal

Sledeća kontrola po planu.Next check as planned.

Blago povišenoSlightly raised

Do 3 puta iznad gornje granice: ponovite za 1–2 nedelje i javite onkologu.Up to 3 times the upper limit: repeat in 1–2 weeks and tell your oncologist.

Prekinite odmahStop at once

Više od 3 puta iznad granice, povišen bilirubin, pad leukocita, žuta koža ili oči, taman urin, smetenost, nesiguran hod, teški osip ili visoka temperatura. Javite se lekaru isti dan.More than 3 times the limit, raised bilirubin, low white cells, yellow skin or eyes, dark urine, confusion, unsteady walking, severe rash or high fever. See a doctor the same day.
