„Hemo mozak“"Chemo brain"

Ne ludite, i to obično prolaziYou're not going crazy, and it usually gets better

Stručni naziv je kognitivno oštećenje povezano sa rakom. Javlja se kod 10% do 75% pacijenata na hemioterapiji. Obično počinje nekoliko dana posle početka terapije, a kod manjeg broja i do 6 meseci kasnije. Dr Roman smatra da se prečesto previdi ili pogrešno nazove demencijom.The medical name is cancer-related cognitive impairment. It affects 10% to 75% of patients on chemotherapy. It usually starts a few days after treatment begins, and in some people up to 6 months later. Dr Roman thinks it is too often missed or mislabelled as dementia.

Kako izgledaWhat it feels like

  • Zaboravljanje imena poznatih ljudi ili gde su ključeviForgetting familiar names or where the keys are
  • Mozak „na usporenom snimku“; za ono što je bilo automatsko sada treba razmišljatiA brain "in slow motion"; what was automatic now takes thought
  • Teška koncentracija, odlutate dok čitatePoor concentration, drifting off while reading
  • Teže rešavanje problema i rad na više stvari odjednomHarder problem solving and multitasking
  • „Magla u glavi“, reč vam je na vrh jezika"Brain fog", the word on the tip of your tongue

Šta povećava rizikWhat raises the risk

  • Neki lekovi: metotreksat, antraciklini (doksorubicin), platina (cisplatin, karboplatin), taksani, 5-FUSome drugs: methotrexate, anthracyclines (doxorubicin), platinum (cisplatin, carboplatin), taxanes, 5-FU
  • Veće doze i kombinacija više lekovaHigher doses and combining several drugs
  • Starije godine i ranije tegobe sa pamćenjemOlder age and earlier memory problems
  • Depresija, hroničan umor, loš san, bol i lekovi protiv bolova (opijati)Depression, chronic fatigue, poor sleep, pain and painkillers (opioids)
  • AlkoholAlcohol

OrganizacijaOrganisation

Podsetnici na telefonu, stvari uvek na istom mestu.Phone reminders, items always in the same place.

KretanjeMovement

Vežbanje značajno poboljšava pažnju i san. Porodica: nemojte terati bolesnika da stalno leži.Exercise markedly improves attention and sleep. Families: don't keep the patient in bed all day.

Meditacija i jogaMeditation and yoga

Meta-analiza 29 randomizovanih studija rangirala je meditaciju kao najdelotvorniju pomoć bez lekova.A meta-analysis of 29 randomised trials ranked meditation the most effective non-drug help.

Trening mozgaBrain training

Ukrštene reči, instrument, novi jezik, čitanje pa prepričavanje nekome. „Ko ne koristi, gubi.“Crosswords, an instrument, a new language, reading and then retelling it. "Use it or lose it."

Pomoć stručnjakaProfessional help

Radna i logopedska terapija, psiholog, kognitivno-bihejvioralna terapija. Psihijatar i psiholog nisu „za lude“.Occupational and speech therapy, a psychologist, cognitive behavioural therapy. Psychiatrists and psychologists are not "for crazy people".

Oprez sa tabletamaCareful with pills

Lekovi za smirenje i neki antidepresivi mogu pogoršati stanje i reagovati sa hemioterapijom. Lekovi za pamćenje (npr. donepezil) su poslednja opcija; standardnog leka nema.Sedatives and some antidepressants can make it worse and interact with chemotherapy. Memory drugs (e.g. donepezil) are a last resort; there is no standard drug.

Performans statusPerformance status

Godine su samo brojAge is just a number

Dr Roman je video pacijente od 60 koji izgledaju gore od nekih od 90. Zato onkolozi ne gledaju godine, nego performans status: koliko ste funkcionalni.Dr Roman has seen patients in their 60s who look worse than some in their 90s. So oncologists look not at age but at performance status: how functional you are.

Šta još lekar gledaWhat else the doctor looks at

  • Rad bubrega, jetre, srca i mozga. Doze mnogih lekova mogu se prilagoditi slabijim bubrezima ili jetri, a lekovi koji opterećuju srce izbeći.Kidney, liver, heart and brain function. Doses of many drugs can be adjusted for weaker kidneys or liver, and heart-straining drugs avoided.
  • Koja je vrsta raka: spora ili agresivna, izlečiva ili ne. Kod izlečivog tumora vredi biti hrabriji.What kind of cancer: slow or aggressive, curable or not. With a curable tumour it's worth being bolder.
  • Ostale bolesti, broj lekova koje uzimate, ishranjenost i podrška kod kuće.Other illnesses, how many medicines you take, nutrition and support at home.

Priča iz ordinacijeA story from the clinic

Sestra je pitala Dr Romana da li je siguran da želi da da hemioterapiju starijem pacijentu u kolicima koji je izgledao užasno. Imao je limfom koji dobro odgovara na terapiju. Posle jedne terapije i hidratacije pacijent se oporavio, a sestra je priznala da je pogrešila.A nurse asked Dr Roman if he was sure about giving chemotherapy to an elderly patient in a wheelchair who looked awful. He had a lymphoma that responds well to treatment. After one treatment and fluids the patient bounced back, and the nurse admitted she had been wrong.

PrilagođavanjeAdjusting

Terapija se kroji po vamaTreatment is tailored to you

Mnoge porodice se iznenade kada čuju koliko toga može da se promeni. Pre nego što kažete „ne“ hemioterapiji, pitajte koje lekove i u kojoj kombinaciji lekar planira; visoka doza cisplatina nije isto što i gemcitabin.Many families are surprised by how much can change. Before saying "no" to chemotherapy, ask which drugs and which combination the doctor plans; high-dose cisplatin is not the same as gemcitabine.

Početi nisko, ići polakoStart low, go slow

Niža početna doza koja se povećava ako je dobro podnosite, ili smanjuje ako ne.A lower starting dose raised if you tolerate it, or lowered if not.

ReđeLess often

Svake druge nedelje umesto svake, pa nedeljno ako ide dobro.Every other week instead of weekly, then weekly if it goes well.

Lek po lekDrug by drug

Početi sa dva leka, pa dodavati treći i četvrti.Start with two drugs, then add a third and fourth.

„Odmor od leka“A "drug holiday"

Pauza od 1–3 nedelje pred krstarenje ili venčanje, pa nastavak.A 1–3 week break before a cruise or wedding, then back on.

Jači lekovi protiv mučnineStronger anti-nausea drugs

Ako vam je muka, postoji mnogo načina da se to ublaži. Recite lekaru.If you're nauseous, there are many ways to ease it. Tell your doctor.

Manje lekovaFewer medicines

Neka lekari pregledaju sve što uzimate i, ako može, uklone ono što nije neophodno. Ne prekidajte ništa sami.Have your doctors review everything you take and remove what isn't essential if possible. Don't stop anything yourself.

Ne upoređujte se sa drugimaDon't compare yourself to others

Dva pacijenta na istoj kombinaciji: jedan kaže „ubijate me“, drugi „osećam se fantastično“. Ono što je bilo komšiji ne mora biti vama.Two patients on the same combination: one says "you're killing me", the other "I feel fabulous". What happened to a neighbour need not happen to you.

Znati kada statiKnowing when to stop

Dođe trenutak kada terapija donosi više štete nego koristi, najčešće kod performans statusa 4. Dr Roman kaže porodicama: odluka nije o vama, nego o njima. Tada je hospicijska nega najbolji izbor.A point comes when treatment does more harm than good, most often at performance status 4. Dr Roman tells families: this decision is not about you, it's about them. Then hospice care is the best choice.

Zračenje sa snimanjaRadiation from scans

Koliko zračenja ste primili?How much radiation have you received?

Zračenje se meri u milisivertima (mSv) i sabira se tokom života. Statistički, rizik od raka počinje da raste iznad oko 100 mSv, kod dece i ranije. Za svakih 10 mSv procenjeni dodatni rizik od smrtonosnog raka je oko 1 na 2.000. Samim životom na Zemlji primimo oko 2,4 mSv godišnje, a let Njujork–London i nazad oko 0,1 mSv.Radiation is measured in millisieverts (mSv) and adds up over a lifetime. Statistically, cancer risk starts to rise above about 100 mSv, earlier in children. For every 10 mSv the estimated extra risk of fatal cancer is about 1 in 2,000. Just living on Earth gives about 2.4 mSv a year, and a New York–London round trip about 0.1 mSv.

Dobra vest za praćenje tumora glave i vrata: MR i ultrazvuk nemaju nikakvo zračenje.Good news for following head and neck tumours: MRI and ultrasound carry no radiation at all.

Saberite svoja snimanjaAdd up your scans

Upišite koliko puta ste imali svako snimanje.Enter how many times you've had each scan.

0 mSv≈ 0 rendgena pluća≈ 0 chest X-rays

Za kateterizaciju i talijum uzeta je srednja vrednost. Primer Dr Romana: CT abdomena (10) + CT glave (2) + 5 mamografija (2) = 14 mSv, veoma malo.For catheterisation and thallium the middle value is used. Dr Roman's example: CT abdomen (10) + CT head (2) + 5 mammograms (2) = 14 mSv, very little.

Ne odbijajte potrebno snimanjeDon't refuse a needed scan

Najveća greška bi bila odbiti važan pregled iz straha od zračenja. Mamografija ima samo 0,4 mSv, a rak dojke dobije svaka osma žena. Pravo pitanje je: „Doktore, da li postoji alternativa, kao MR ili ultrazvuk?“ Ako je ovo snimanje najbolje, korist je veća od rizika. Kod dece budite posebno oprezni.The biggest mistake would be refusing an important test out of fear of radiation. A mammogram is only 0.4 mSv, and one in eight women gets breast cancer. The right question is: "Doctor, is there an alternative, such as MRI or ultrasound?" If this scan is the best one, the benefit outweighs the risk. Be extra careful with children.
