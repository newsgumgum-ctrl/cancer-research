Hrana kao lekFood as medicine

Podignite štitove telaRaise your body's shields

Dr William Li

Dr William Li je lekar i naučnik koji je pomogao u razvoju lekova odobrenih za preko 70 bolesti. U laboratoriji je istu metodu kojom se traže lekovi primenio na hranu, i iznenadio se. Na ovoj stranici je ono što preporučuje, uz njegovu glavnu poruku: volite hranu koju jedete.Dr William Li is a physician and scientist who helped develop treatments approved for more than 70 diseases. In the lab he tested foods with the same method used to find drugs, and was surprised. This page covers what he recommends, with his main message: love the food you eat.

Odbrambeni sistemiHealth defences

Svi stalno stvaramo mikroskopske tumoreWe all form microscopic cancers all the time

Dr Li objašnjava da telo ima oko 40 biliona ćelija i da se pri deljenju svakog dana napravi oko 10.000 grešaka u DNK. Svaka je početak mikroskopskog tumora. Ne obolevamo stalno jer je telo opremljeno odbrambenim sistemima koji ih pronalaze i uklanjaju, kao policijska patrola u mirnom kraju.Dr Li explains that the body has about 40 trillion cells and that around 10,000 DNA copying mistakes slip through every day. Each one is the start of a microscopic cancer. We don't get sick all the time because the body is hardwired with health defences that find and remove them, like a police patrol in a quiet neighbourhood.

10.000grešaka u DNK dnevno, koje telo ne ispraviDNA mistakes a day that the body doesn't fix
1 cmnajmanji tumor koji se napipa, već oko milijardu ćelijasmallest tumour you can feel, already about a billion cells
16.000×koliko tumor naraste za dve nedelje kad ga dotakne krvni sud (u laboratoriji)how much a tumour grows in two weeks once a blood vessel reaches it (in the lab)
96.000 kmkrvnih sudova u telu odrasle osobe (60.000 milja)of blood vessels in an adult body (60,000 miles)

Imunski sistemImmune system

Patrolira telom i uklanja abnormalne ćelije.Patrols the body and removes abnormal cells.

AngiogenezaAngiogenesis

Kontroliše krvne sudove: hrani zdrava tkiva, a ne dozvoljava im da hrane tumor. Li to zove zona Zlatokose: ni previše, ni premalo.Controls blood vessels: feeds healthy tissue but keeps them from feeding tumours. Li calls it the Goldilocks zone: not too much, not too little.

DNK, matične ćelije i crevna floraDNA, stem cells and gut bacteria

Popravka DNK, obnova tkiva iz matičnih ćelija i bakterije u crevima čine ostale štitove.DNA repair, tissue renewal from stem cells and gut bacteria make up the other shields.

Šta spušta štitoveWhat lowers the shields

Ne povremeno, nego svakodnevnoNot once in a while, but every day

Li kaže da hamburger ili brza hrana povremeno ne škode ako su odbrambeni sistemi jaki. Problem je navika.Li says a burger or fast food once in a while won't harm you if your defences are strong. The problem is the habit.

SoSalt

Ubrzava starenje ćelija i troši krvne sudove; restoranska hrana je često veoma slana.Speeds cellular ageing and wears down blood vessels; restaurant food is often very salty.

Dodati šećerAdded sugar

Stalno visok šećer iscrpljuje metabolizam. Šećer iz voća nije problem.Constantly high sugar wears out the metabolism. Sugar from fruit is not the problem.

AlkoholAlcohol

Otrov za mozak, jetru i srce. Korisne materije iz crvenog grožđa nisu u alkoholu.Toxic to the brain, liver and heart. The useful substances in red grapes are not in the alcohol.

Hronični stresChronic stress

Slabi imunitet, diže pritisak i kvari san. Malo stresa je dobro; stres bez kraja nije.Weakens immunity, raises blood pressure and ruins sleep. A little stress is good; endless stress is not.

Loš sanPoor sleep

U dubokom snu mozak se čisti (glimfatički sistem) i telo sagoreva mast. Cilj je oko 8 sati.In deep sleep the brain cleans itself (the glymphatic system) and the body burns fat. Aim for about 8 hours.

Prerađeno mesoProcessed meat

Salama, kobasice, delikatesno meso: Svetska zdravstvena organizacija ih smatra kancerogenim, posebno za rak debelog creva.Salami, sausages, deli meats: the World Health Organization considers them carcinogenic, especially for bowel cancer.

MikroplastikaMicroplastics

Plastika koju nesvesno jedemoThe plastic we eat without knowing

Li navodi nalaze mikroplastike u mozgu (u proseku kao jedna plastična kašičica), krvi, majčinom mleku, testisima i semenu. U jednoj italijanskoj studiji muškarci sa plastikom u zidu karotidne arterije imali su četiri puta veći rizik od smrtonosnog srčanog ili moždanog udara. Neke procene kažu da ljudi pojedu i do jedne kreditne kartice plastike nedeljno, mada je ta računica osporavana.Li cites findings of microplastics in the brain (on average about one plastic spoon's worth), blood, breast milk, testicles and semen. In an Italian study, men with plastic in their carotid artery wall had four times the risk of a fatal heart attack or stroke. Some estimates say people eat up to a credit card's worth of plastic a week, though that math has been challenged.

Bacite plastično posuđeBin the plastic tableware

Plastične čaše, tanjire i pribor zamenite keramikom i staklom.Swap plastic cups, plates and cutlery for ceramic and glass.

Hrana bez plastikeFood without plastic

Izbegavajte hranu upakovanu u plastiku i čuvanje ostataka u plastičnim posudama.Avoid food packed in plastic and storing leftovers in plastic boxes.

Čaj bez kesiceTea without the bag

Istraživanje iz Montreala: jedna kesica čaja može otpustiti milijardu čestica plastike. Bolje rinfuzni čaj ili matča.Research from Montreal: one tea bag can shed a billion plastic particles. Loose-leaf tea or matcha is better.

Pametne kombinacijeSmart pairings

Isto jelo, više koristiSame food, more benefit

Li pokazuje da način pripreme menja koliko korisnih materija telo zaista upije.Li shows that how you prepare food changes how much of the good stuff your body actually absorbs.

Paradajz + maslinovo uljeTomato + olive oil

Likopen iz sirovog paradajza telo upije oko 20%. Kada se paradajz kratko prodinsta na maslinovom ulju, upija se oko 80%, jer se likopen rastvara u mastima. Isto važi za salsu sa gvakamoleom.The body absorbs about 20% of the lycopene in raw tomato. Sautéed briefly in olive oil, about 80% is absorbed, because lycopene dissolves in fat. The same goes for salsa with guacamole.

Kurkuma + crni biberTurmeric + black pepper

Kurkumin iz kurkume telo slabo zadržava. Piperin iz sveže mlevenog crnog bibera pomaže telu da ga zadrži.The body holds on poorly to curcumin from turmeric. Piperine in freshly cracked black pepper helps it hold on.

Čaj bez kravljeg mlekaTea without cow's milk

Masnoća iz kravljeg mleka pravi sitne mehuriće koji zarobe korisne katehine iz čaja, pa ih telo izbaci. Biljna mleka (bademovo, indijski orah, soja) nemaju taj efekat. Postoji i tajvanski „mlečni čaj“ (ulong) koji prirodno ima ukus mleka.Fat in cow's milk forms tiny bubbles that trap tea's helpful catechins, so the body flushes them out. Plant milks (almond, cashew, soy) don't do that. There is also a Taiwanese "milk tea" (oolong) that naturally tastes milky.

Pečurke + sunceMushrooms + sunlight

Isecite obične bele pečurke na tanke listiće i ostavite ih par sati na sunčanom prozoru pre kuvanja. Napraviće više vitamina D.Slice plain white mushrooms thinly and leave them on a sunny windowsill for a couple of hours before cooking. They will make more vitamin D.

NamirniceFoods

Šta Dr Li izdvajaWhat Dr Li singles out

Li je proučio više od 200 namirnica. U laboratoriji su mnoge, poput zelenog čaja, luka, belog luka, crvenog grožđa i jagoda, smanjivale rast krvnih sudova koji bi hranili tumor, kao i lekovi protiv raka. Neka zapažanja su iz velikih studija na ljudima, a neka iz laboratorije.Li has studied more than 200 foods. In the lab many, such as green tea, onions, garlic, red grapes and strawberries, cut back the blood vessel growth that would feed a tumour, just like cancer drugs. Some findings come from large human studies, others from the lab.

NamirnicaFoodŠta kaže Dr LiWhat Dr Li says
Kuvani paradajzCooked tomatoesU velikoj studiji zdravstvenih radnika, muškarci koji jedu kuvani paradajz 2–3 puta nedeljno (po pola šolje) imali su 29% manji rizik od raka prostate. Kod onih koji su ipak oboleli, tumori su imali manje krvnih sudova i bili manje agresivni.In a large study of health professionals, men eating cooked tomatoes 2–3 times a week (half a cup each) had a 29% lower risk of prostate cancer. In those who still got it, tumours had fewer blood vessels and were less aggressive.
Zeleni čaj i matčaGreen tea and matchaKatehini smanjuju upalu i stres, a u laboratoriji su ubijali matične ćelije raka dojke. Matča je ceo mleveni list, pa daje sve polifenole i vlakna. Njegov stric je doživeo 104 godine pijući oko 10 šoljica zelenog čaja dnevno.Catechins lower inflammation and stress, and in the lab killed breast cancer stem cells. Matcha is the whole ground leaf, so you get all the polyphenols plus fibre. His great-uncle lived to 104 drinking about 10 cups of green tea a day.
Earl Grey čajEarl Grey teaU njihovom testu krvnih sudova bio je najjači od svih čajeva, verovatno zbog bergamota.In their blood vessel test it was the most potent of all the teas, perhaps because of bergamot.
KafaCoffeeHlorogenska kiselina smanjuje upalu i budi smeđe masno tkivo koje sagoreva štetnu mast. Doza iz studija: 3–4 šoljice dnevno.Chlorogenic acid lowers inflammation and wakes up brown fat, which burns harmful fat. The dose in studies: 3–4 cups a day.
Ljubičasti krompirPurple potatoesAntocijani su u laboratoriji (Penn State) ubijali matične ćelije raka debelog creva.Anthocyanins killed bowel cancer stem cells in the lab (Penn State).
Bobičasto voćeBerriesBorovnice, jagode, maline. Maline su po težini među namirnicama sa najviše vlakana.Blueberries, strawberries, raspberries. Raspberries are among the most fibre-rich foods by weight.
Orašasti plodoviTree nutsOrasi, bademi, makadamija, pistaći: vlakna, proteini, zdrave masti.Walnuts, almonds, macadamia, pistachios: fibre, protein, healthy fats.
Tamna čokolada, cvekla, spanaćDark chocolate, beetroot, spinachPodstiču azot-monoksid, koji širi krvne sudove i privlači zdrave matične ćelije, važno i za mozak.Boost nitric oxide, which widens blood vessels and recruits healthy stem cells, important for the brain too.
Lisnato povrćeLeafy greensBok čoj, kelj, radič, eskarola: osnova mediteranske i azijske kuhinje.Bok choy, kale, chicory, escarole: staples of Mediterranean and Asian cooking.

Pet koje bi Dr Li poneo na pusto ostrvoDr Li's five desert-island foods

Kafa i čaj, orašasti plodovi, paradajz, bobičasto voće i lisnato zeleno povrće.Coffee and tea, tree nuts, tomatoes, berries and leafy greens.

ImunoterapijaImmunotherapy

Kraj rata protiv raka dolazi od imunskog sistemaThe end of the war on cancer comes from the immune system

Li kaže da je video više od desetak pacijenata kako idu od stadijuma 4 do stadijuma 0. Ne kod svih, ali vidi se svetlo na kraju tunela.Li says he has seen more than a dozen patients go from stage 4 to stage 0. Not for everyone yet, but there is light at the end of the tunnel.

Njegova majkaHis mother

U 80. godini dobila je rak sluznice materice koji se posle operacije proširio, stadijum 4. Primila je tri doze imunoterapije u razmaku od tri nedelje, uz malo zračenja i potpuno prilagođenu ishranu. Posle devet nedelja snimak je pokazao stadijum 0, bez hemioterapije. Deset godina kasnije, sa 90 godina, nema raka.At 80 she had endometrial cancer that spread after surgery, stage 4. She had three doses of immunotherapy three weeks apart, a little radiation and a completely adjusted diet. After nine weeks scans showed stage 0, without chemotherapy. Ten years later, at 90, she is cancer-free.

Bakterija AkkermansiaThe Akkermansia bacterium

Dr Laurence Zitvogel (Institut Gustave Roussy, Pariz) je 2017. pokazala da su pacijenti koji odgovaraju na imunoterapiju imali jednu bakteriju u crevima: Akkermansia muciniphila. Prenošenjem te bakterije miševima vraćen je odgovor na terapiju. Rast joj pomažu nar, brusnica, grožđe Concord, ljuta paprika i kinesko crno sirće; postoji i kao probiotik.In 2017 Dr Laurence Zitvogel (Gustave Roussy Institute, Paris) showed that patients who responded to immunotherapy had one gut bacterium: Akkermansia muciniphila. Giving that bacterium to mice restored their response. Pomegranate, cranberry, Concord grape, chilli peppers and Chinese black vinegar help it grow; it is also sold as a probiotic.

Li navodi i personalizovane vakcine od proteina sopstvenog tumora (dr Saskia Biskup u Nemačkoj), sa obećavajućim rezultatima kod glioblastoma, i primer Rebeke Divajn koja sedam godina živi sa glioblastomom.Li also mentions personalised vaccines made from proteins of a person's own tumour (Dr Saskia Biskup in Germany), with promising results in glioblastoma, and the example of Rebecca Devine, living with glioblastoma for seven years.

Visceralna mastVisceral fat

I vitka osoba može imati opasnu mastEven a slim person can have dangerous fat

Mast je organ, i korisna je. Opasna je mast koja se nakuplja unutar trbuha, oko organa. Kada preraste svoje krvne sudove, ostaje bez kiseonika, upali se i „curi“ upalu po telu, kao kesica kreme koja pukne u koferu. Upala na tumor deluje kao benzin na žar.Fat is an organ, and it is useful. The dangerous fat builds up inside the belly, around the organs. When it outgrows its blood supply it runs short of oxygen, becomes inflamed and "leaks" inflammation through the body, like a tube of cream bursting in a suitcase. On a tumour, inflammation acts like petrol on embers.

3× veći rizik3× the risk

Švedska studija sa oko 3.000 žena normalne težine: one sa viškom unutrašnje masti imale su za 13 godina tri puta veći rizik od raka dojke. Visceralna mast je povezana sa 14 vrsta raka.A Swedish study of about 3,000 normal-weight women: those with excess internal fat had three times the breast cancer risk over 13 years. Visceral fat is linked to 14 cancers.

Smeđa mast pomažeBrown fat helps

Tanki sloj oko vrata, iza grudne kosti i između lopatica sagoreva štetnu mast. Budi je hladnoća (hladnija soba za spavanje, hladna kupka) i kafa.A thin layer around the neck, behind the breastbone and between the shoulder blades burns harmful fat. Cold wakes it up (a cooler bedroom, a cold plunge), and so does coffee.

Hara hači buHara hachi bu

Japanska izreka: prestanite da jedete kada ste 80% siti. Li veruje da je neprejedanje jedan od razloga dugovečnosti Japanaca.A Japanese saying: stop eating when you are 80% full. Li believes not overeating is one reason the Japanese live so long.

Način životaWay of life

Kako Dr Li jedeHow Dr Li eats

Mediteranski stil, ne dijetaMediterranean style, not a diet

Većinu popularnih dijeta (keto, karnivor, sirova hrana) Li smatra kratkoročnim rešenjima koja se ne mogu održati ceo život. Sam jede mediteranski: biljna, sezonska, sveža hrana i zdrava ulja, kao i u zdravim azijskim kuhinjama.Li sees most popular diets (keto, carnivore, raw food) as short-term fixes you can't keep up for life. He eats Mediterranean-style: plant-forward, seasonal, fresh food and healthy oils, as in healthy Asian cooking.

Prirodni post od 12 satiA natural 12-hour fast

  1. Posle večere u 20 h ništa više ne jedite.After dinner at 8 pm, no more eating.
  2. Spavajte od 23 do 7 h (8 sati).Sleep from 11 pm to 7 am (8 hours).
  3. Posle buđenja sačekajte sat vremena pre doručka.After waking, wait an hour before breakfast.

To je 12 sati posta bez muke. Ko želi 16 sati, preskoči doručak, ali se ne prejeda za ručak.That's 12 hours of fasting with no struggle. For 16 hours, skip breakfast but don't overeat at lunch.

Suplementi kao „dopuna“Supplements as a "top-up"

Prvo hrana, jer cela namirnica nosi stotine korisnih materija. Li ipak uzima vitamin D3, omega-3 masne kiseline, probiotik Akkermansia i probiotik Lactobacillus reuteri (žvakaći, koji štiti i zube).Food first, because a whole food carries hundreds of helpful substances. Still, Li takes vitamin D3, omega-3 fatty acids, an Akkermansia probiotic and a chewable Lactobacillus reuteri probiotic (which also protects teeth).

Njegova glavna porukaHis main message

Volite hranu da biste voleli zdravlje. Od 200 zdravih namirnica zaokružite one koje već volite i krenite od njih. Tako ste već ispred.Love your food to love your health. Of the 200 healthy foods, circle the ones you already love and start with those. You're already ahead.
