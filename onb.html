Olfaktorni neuroblastom, korak po korak Olfactory neuroblastoma, one step at a time

Retka bolest ne mora da znači usamljenost. Ovde smo skupili proverene informacije o bolesti, lečenju i životu posle lečenja, jednostavnim rečima i sa izvorima.

A rare disease does not have to mean facing it alone. Here we gathered checked information about the disease, its treatment and life afterwards, in plain words and with sources.

Stanford Health Care writes that most people with olfactory neuroblastoma are cured with treatment. The numbers on this site are averages from studies. Your doctor knows your case.

BolestThe disease

Šta je olfaktorni neuroblastom?What is olfactory neuroblastoma?

Olfaktorni neuroblastom (drugo ime: estezioneuroblastom, skraćeno ONB) je redak tumor koji nastaje visoko u nosu, iz ćelija zaduženih za njuh. To mesto je odmah ispod tanke koštane pločice koja liči na sito (lamina cribrosa). Kroz njene rupice nervna vlakna za njuh idu do mozga. Zato je blizina mozga i oka važna za plan lečenja.

Olfactory neuroblastoma (also called esthesioneuroblastoma, or ONB) is a rare tumour that starts high up in the nose, in the cells that sense smell. That spot sits right below a thin plate of bone shaped like a sieve (the cribriform plate). Smell nerve fibres pass through its tiny holes to the brain. That is why the nearby brain and eye matter when treatment is planned.

Česti prvi znaciCommon first signs

  • Zapušen nos, često samo s jedne straneBlocked nose, often on one side only
  • Krvarenje iz nosaNosebleeds
  • Slabiji ili izgubljen njuh, ponekad godinama pre dijagnozeWeaker or lost sense of smell, sometimes for years before diagnosis
  • Kod većih tumora: promene vida, otok lica, glavoboljaWith larger tumours: vision changes, facial swelling, headache

Znaci liče na prehladu ili sinusitis, pa od prvih tegoba do dijagnoze obično prođe 6–12 meseci.The signs look like a cold or sinusitis, so it usually takes 6–12 months from first symptoms to diagnosis.

Kako se postavlja dijagnozaHow it is diagnosed

  • ORL lekar pregleda nos kamerom (endoskopija)An ENT doctor looks inside the nose with a camera (endoscopy)
  • Uzima se mali uzorak tkiva (biopsija)A small tissue sample is taken (biopsy)
  • MR i CT pokazuju veličinu tumora i odnos prema oku i mozguMRI and CT show the tumour's size and how close it is to the eye and brain
  • Proveravaju se i limfni čvorovi vrataThe lymph nodes in the neck are checked too

Za ovaj tumor nisu poznati faktori rizika. Niste ništa pogrešno uradili.No risk factors are known for this tumour. You did nothing wrong.

Stadijum i gradusStage and grade

Dve oznake koje ćete viđati u nalazimaTwo labels you will see in your reports

Stadijum govori koliko se tumor proširio. Gradus govori kako ćelije izgledaju pod mikroskopom. Obe oznake zajedno pomažu lekarima da izaberu lečenje.

Stage tells how far the tumour has spread. Grade tells how the cells look under the microscope. Together they help doctors choose treatment.

LečenjeTreatment

Kako izgleda lečenjeWhat treatment looks like

Osnova lečenja je operacija. Posle nje često sledi zračenje. Redosled i kombinaciju bira tim lekara za svakog pacijenta posebno.

Surgery is the foundation of treatment. Radiotherapy often follows. A team of doctors chooses the order and combination for each patient.

  1. OperacijaSurgery

    Danas najčešće kroz nos, kamerom (endoskopski), bez reza na licu. Kod velikih tumora kombinuje se sa otvorenom operacijom.Today usually done through the nose with a camera (endoscopic), with no cut on the face. Large tumours may need open surgery as well.

  2. ZračenjeRadiotherapy

    Najčešće počinje 4–6 nedelja posle operacije, pet dana nedeljno oko 6 nedelja. Uobičajena doza je 50–60 Gy.Usually starts 4–6 weeks after surgery, five days a week for about 6 weeks. The usual dose is 50–60 Gy.

  3. Hemioterapija, ako trebaChemotherapy, if needed

    Uglavnom kada se tumor proširio ka mozgu, oku ili limfnim čvorovima, ili kada se vrati.Mainly when the tumour has spread towards the brain, eye or lymph nodes, or when it comes back.

  4. PraćenjeFollow-up

    Redovni MR i pregledi, dugo godina. Više u sledećem delu.Regular MRI scans and check-ups for many years. More in the next section.

Nova mogućnostA newer option

SSTR2: „brava“ na tumorskim ćelijamaSSTR2: a "lock" on the tumour cells

Mnogi olfaktorni neuroblastomi na površini imaju receptor SSTR2. Ako patolog u nalazu napiše „SSTR2 pozitivan“, to otvara dve mogućnosti. Prva je posebno snimanje (Ga-68 DOTATATE ili DOTATOC PET) koje pokazuje gde se tumor nalazi. Druga je ciljano zračenje iznutra (PRRT, Lutecijum-177 DOTATATE), za tumore koji se vrate ili prošire.

Many olfactory neuroblastomas carry the SSTR2 receptor on their surface. If your pathology report says "SSTR2 positive", two options open up. One is a special scan (Ga-68 DOTATATE or DOTATOC PET) that shows where the tumour is. The other is targeted radiation from inside the body (PRRT, Lutetium-177 DOTATATE) for tumours that come back or spread.

PRRT je za ovaj tumor opisan u manjim serijama i prikazima slučajeva. U jednom prikazu iz 2025. godine pacijentkinja sa proširenom bolešću primila je 4 ciklusa i bolest je bila pod kontrolom oko 12 meseci, uz blage neželjene efekte. To nije standardna terapija, ali je dobro pitanje za vašeg onkologa.

PRRT for this tumour has been described in small series and case reports. In a 2025 case report, a woman with spread disease received 4 cycles and her disease stayed under control for about 12 months, with mild side effects. It is not standard treatment yet, but it is a good question for your oncologist.

Kliničko istraživanjeResearch study

Studija Nacionalnog instituta za rak (SAD)US National Cancer Institute study

NIH u Betezdi (SAD) vodi studiju prirodnog toka bolesti kod dece i odraslih sa olfaktornim neuroblastomom (NCT04755205). Učešće je besplatno. Studija prati simptome, tok bolesti i odgovor na lečenje. Pre prijave proverite da li još prima pacijente.

The NIH in Bethesda (USA) runs a natural history study of children and adults with olfactory neuroblastoma (NCT04755205). Taking part is free. The study follows symptoms, how the disease behaves and how it responds to treatment. Check whether it is still enrolling before applying.

PraćenjeFollow-up

Zašto je praćenje dugoWhy follow-up lasts so long

Ovaj tumor ume da bude strpljiv. Kada se vrati, to se često desi tek posle 5 ili više godina. Zato se praćenje ne završava posle pet godina, kao kod mnogih drugih tumora.

This tumour can be patient. When it returns, it often does so only after 5 years or more. That is why follow-up does not stop at five years, as it does for many other cancers.

Dobro je znatiGood to know
  • Povratak bolesti se javlja kod 30–60% pacijenata, ali se često ponovo uspešno leči.The disease returns in 30–60% of patients, but it can often be treated successfully again.
  • Kasni čvorovi na vratu su česti i obično se uspešno leče kada se otkriju na vreme.Late lymph nodes in the neck are common and are usually treated successfully when found early.
  • Kod visokog gradusa bolest se u proseku vraća ranije nego kod niskog.High-grade tumours tend to return sooner than low-grade ones.
Šta tražiti od lekaraWhat to ask your doctor for
  • Pisani plan praćenja za narednih 10 i više godinaA written follow-up plan for the next 10 years or more
  • MR uvek u istoj ustanovi i istom tehnikom (tanki preseci), da bi se snimci mogli poreditiMRI in the same centre with the same technique (thin slices), so scans can be compared
  • Pregled vrata (palpacija ili ultrazvuk) uz svaki MRA neck check (exam or ultrasound) with each MRI
  • Posle zračenja baze lobanje: povremena provera hormona hipofizeAfter skull-base radiotherapy: periodic pituitary hormone tests

An anonymous example of one woman. Every case is different, but it helps to see that someone has already walked the same road.

    Mesecima zapušena desna nozdrva, bol u licu i krvarenje iz nosa. Prvo se mislilo na polip.Months of a blocked right nostril, facial pain and nosebleeds. At first it looked like a polyp.

  2. ~6. mesec~month 6

    CT i MR, pa endoskopska operacija. Dijagnoza: olfaktorni neuroblastom, Hyams gradus I, SSTR2 pozitivan. Deo tumora nije mogao bezbedno da se ukloni.CT and MRI, then endoscopic surgery. Diagnosis: olfactory neuroblastoma, Hyams grade I, SSTR2 positive. Part of the tumour could not be removed safely.

  3. +2 meseca+2 months

    Zračenje: 30 dana zračenja, ukupno 60 Gy. Neželjeni efekti su bili blagi (iritacija sluzokože i kože).Radiotherapy: 30 sessions, 60 Gy in total. Side effects were mild (irritated lining of the nose and skin).

  4. +8 meseci+8 months

    Prvi MR posle zračenja: ostatak tumora oko 65% manji nego pre lečenja.First MRI after radiotherapy: the remaining tumour about 65% smaller than before treatment.

  5. +17 meseci+17 months

    Ostatak se i dalje polako smanjuje, bez širenja. Opšte stanje dobro, svakodnevni život bez većih tegoba.The remnant keeps shrinking slowly, with no spread. Feeling well, daily life without major complaints.

    Stabilno. Redovan MR na nekoliko meseci, uz ishranu, kretanje i pitanja koja beleži za svaku kontrolu. Ovaj sajt je nastao iz tih pitanja.Stable. Regular MRI every few months, along with good food, movement and a list of questions for each check-up. This site grew out of those questions.

Pitanja za lekaraQuestions for your doctor

Ponesite ova pitanja na kontroluTake these questions to your next visit

Kopirajte listu u telefon ili je prepišite. Lekari vole pripremljene pacijente.

Copy the list to your phone or write it down. Doctors appreciate prepared patients.

  • Koji je tačan stadijum i gradus mog tumora?
  • Da li je tumor SSTR2 pozitivan i da li bi mi koristio DOTATATE PET?
  • Koliko dugo i koliko često ću raditi MR? Može li uvek u istoj ustanovi, sa tankim presecima?
  • Da li treba pratiti limfne čvorove vrata ultrazvukom?
  • Treba li da proveravam hormone hipofize posle zračenja?
  • Da li je moj slučaj razmatran u centru za tumore baze lobanje? Mogu li dobiti drugo mišljenje?
  • Postoje li kliničke studije za koje sam kandidat?
  • Koji suplementi su bezbedni uz moju terapiju?
  • Imate li preporuku za nutricionistu i psihoonkologa?
  • What exactly are the stage and grade of my tumour?
  • Is the tumour SSTR2 positive, and would a DOTATATE PET scan help me?
  • How long and how often will I have MRI scans? Can they always be done in the same centre, with thin slices?
  • Should my neck lymph nodes be checked with ultrasound?
  • Should my pituitary hormones be checked after radiotherapy?
  • Has my case been reviewed at a skull-base tumour centre? Can I get a second opinion?
  • Are there clinical trials I might be eligible for?
  • Which supplements are safe with my treatment?
  • Can you refer me to a dietitian and a psycho-oncologist?
