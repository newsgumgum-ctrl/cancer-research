Ishrana na terapijiEating during treatment

Jedite dovoljno da biste ostali jakiEat enough to stay strong

Dr Roman

Dr Roman je za 19 godina rada sa pacijentima na terapiji slušao šta im prija, a šta ne. Ovde su njegovi praktični saveti, kao da sedite kod njega u ordinaciji.Over 19 years Dr Roman has listened to what patients in treatment tolerate and what they don't. Here is his practical advice, as if you were sitting in his office.

Najčešća greškaThe most common mistake

Posle dijagnoze mnogi pređu na veoma strogu „zdravu“ ishranu ili post. Izgube mnogo kilograma, oslabe i lošije podnose operaciju, hemioterapiju, zračenje i imunoterapiju. Ponekad lečenje mora da se prekine ili promeni. Jedite zdravo, ali jedite.After diagnosis many switch to a very strict "healthy" diet or fasting. They lose a lot of weight, grow weak and tolerate surgery, chemotherapy, radiation and immunotherapy worse. Sometimes treatment has to be paused or changed. Eat healthy, but eat.

OsnoveThe basics

Četiri stvari koje treba znatiFour things to know

Ne postoji jedna najbolja ishranaThere is no single best diet

Svaki pacijent je drugačiji. Ono što prija jednom ne mora drugom.Every patient is different. What works for one may not work for another.

Višak kilograma nije isto što i dobra ishranjenostBeing overweight isn't being well-nourished

I gojazna osoba može biti pothranjena.An overweight person can still be malnourished.

Imunitet je često slabijiImmunity is often lower

Tokom terapije telo teže brani od infekcija, pa je važna i ishrana i higijena hrane.During treatment the body fights infection less well, so both nutrition and food hygiene matter.

Pitajte za nutricionistuAsk for a dietitian

Ako možete, posetite dijetetičara. Oni se time bave svaki dan.If you can, see a registered dietitian. This is what they do every day.

ProteiniProtein

Koliko proteina vam trebaHow much protein you need

Zdravoj odrasloj osobi treba oko 0,8 g proteina po kilogramu telesne težine dnevno. Tokom lečenja raka potreba raste na 1,2–1,5 g po kilogramu. Gornja vrednost je za one koji su slabiji ili imaju jaču terapiju (operacija, hemioterapija, zračenje, imunoterapija). Rasporedite proteine kroz ceo dan.A healthy adult needs about 0.8 g of protein per kilogram of body weight a day. During cancer treatment that rises to 1.2–1.5 g per kilogram. The upper end is for people who are weaker or on heavier treatment (surgery, chemotherapy, radiation, immunotherapy). Spread protein through the day.

Proteinski napici iz apoteke su u redu, ali prvo jedite, pa tek onda dopunite napitkom. Zato se i zovu dodaci.Protein shakes from the pharmacy are fine, but eat first and then top up with a shake. That's why they're called supplements.

Jaja su, kaže Dr Roman, jedna od namirnica koju pacijenti na hemioterapiji najčešće hvale da dobro vare, posebno belanca.Eggs, Dr Roman says, are one of the foods patients on chemotherapy most often say they digest well, especially egg whites.

Voće i povrćeFruit and vegetables

Važnije je oprati nego skuvatiWashing matters more than cooking

Ranije se mislilo da povrće tokom terapije mora biti dobro kuvano. Danas se zna da je najvažnije pranje, jer na površini mogu biti pesticidi i bakterije.It used to be thought that vegetables must be well cooked during treatment. Today we know washing matters most, because the surface can carry pesticides and bacteria.

  1. Čiste rukeClean hands

    Prvo operite ruke.Wash your hands first.

  2. Hladna tekuća vodaCool running water

    Ispirajte pod mlazom vode iz česme. Sapun i deterdžent ne pomažu, a mogu da iznerviraju stomak.Rinse under running tap water. Soap and detergent don't help and can upset your stomach.

  3. Meka četkicaSoft brush

    Izribajte površinu. Oštećene delove isecite tek posle pranja.Scrub the surface. Cut away damaged spots only after washing.

  4. ObrišitePat dry

    Čistim papirnim ubrusom, što skida još malo ostataka.With a clean paper towel, which removes a little more residue.

Sirće ili limun?Vinegar or lemon?

Rezultati studija su neujednačeni. Sirće možda pomaže kod hrapavih površina (zelena salata, krastavac), a kod glatkih (paradajz, jabuka) ne dodaje mnogo u odnosu na vodu.Study results are mixed. Vinegar may help with rough surfaces (lettuce, cucumber), but adds little over water for smooth ones (tomatoes, apples).

Soda bikarbonaBaking soda

Kod pesticida jedino ona pokazuje neku korist, ali tek posle 12–15 minuta potapanja, i ne skida ono što je ušlo u koru. Posle toga ipak isperite. Dr Roman tako potapa borovnice.For pesticides it is the only one with some benefit, but only after 12–15 minutes of soaking, and it doesn't remove what has entered the peel. Rinse afterwards anyway. Dr Roman soaks blueberries this way.

Šta pacijenti dobro podnoseWhat patients tolerate well

Iz iskustva pacijenata Dr RomanaFrom Dr Roman's patients' experience

Pasulj: „pun pogodak“Beans: "a home run"

Oko 99% njegovih pacijenata kaže da dobro vare mahunarke, da im pomažu da dobiju na težini i da redovno idu u toalet: sočivo, leblebija, crni, crveni i šareni pasulj, grašak, edamame, humus (pazite na začine u gotovom humusu).About 99% of his patients say they digest legumes well, that they help them gain weight and stay regular: lentils, chickpeas, black, kidney and pinto beans, split peas, edamame, hummus (watch the seasonings in shop-bought hummus).

PovrćeVegetables

Brokoli, karfiol, kupus, prokelj, kelj, spanać, zelena salata, šargarepa, slatki krompir, bundeva, beli i crni luk, praziluk, paprika, pečurke, špargla, boranija, cvekla, krastavac, tikvice. Paradajz se bolje upija kuvan, ali kod nekih izaziva gorušicu.Broccoli, cauliflower, cabbage, Brussels sprouts, kale, spinach, romaine, carrots, sweet potato, squash, pumpkin, garlic, onions, leeks, peppers, mushrooms, asparagus, green beans, beets, cucumber, courgette. Tomatoes absorb better cooked, but give some people heartburn.

VoćeFruit

Bobičasto voće, jabuke, kruške, banane, grožđe, dinja, breskve, šljive, trešnje, kivi, avokado. Dinja i medunica umiruju ranice u ustima. Citrusi mogu da peku ranice od hemioterapije.Berries, apples, pears, bananas, grapes, melon, peaches, plums, cherries, kiwi, avocado. Cantaloupe and honeydew soothe mouth sores. Citrus can sting chemo mouth sores.

OstaloOther

Integralne žitarice (najmanje 30 g vlakana dnevno), čak i obične kokice bez putera; nesoljeni orašasti plodovi i semenke; riba (ako miris ne izaziva mučninu); piletina, ćuretina; dobro kuvana jaja; nemasni mlečni proizvodi; maslinovo i repičino ulje. Soja umereno, oko 3 porcije dnevno, i kod žena koje su imale rak dojke.Whole grains (at least 30 g of fibre a day), even plain popcorn without butter; unsalted nuts and seeds; fish (if the smell doesn't cause nausea); chicken, turkey; well-cooked eggs; low-fat dairy; olive and canola oil. Soy in moderation, about 3 servings a day, including for women with a history of breast cancer.

Grejpfrut: mnogi lekovi protiv raka, naročito tablete, razgrađuju se u jetri preko enzima koje grejpfrut koči. Nivo leka može da poraste do toksičnog. Pitajte lekara pre nego što ga jedete ili pijete.Grapefruit: many cancer drugs, especially pills, are broken down in the liver by enzymes that grapefruit blocks. Drug levels can rise to toxic. Ask your doctor before eating or drinking it.

Tečnost i mučninaFluids and nausea

Hidratacija je ogromna stvarHydration is huge

Ako dehidrirate zbog povraćanja, proliva ili malo pića, bićete veoma loše. Pratite boju mokraće: ako je tamna, pijete premalo. Pitajte lekara da li možete da dođete na infuziju u ordinaciju; pacijenti koji uđu u kolicima često izađu kao drugi ljudi posle litra fiziološkog rastvora.If you get dehydrated from vomiting, diarrhoea or drinking too little, you will feel very ill. Watch your urine colour: if it's dark, you're drinking too little. Ask your doctor whether you can come in for IV fluids; patients who arrive in a wheelchair often leave a different person after a litre of saline.

PijteDrink

Voda pre svega. Nezaslađen čaj i crna kafa su uglavnom u redu. Hladni napici smanjuju mučninu.Water above all. Unsweetened tea and black coffee are mostly fine. Cold drinks ease nausea.

OgraničiteLimit

Gazirana i zaslađena pića, kupovni sokovi i limunada (pročitajte koliko šećera imaju). Veoma vrući napici iritiraju jednjak i pojačavaju mučninu.Fizzy and sweetened drinks, shop-bought juices and lemonade (read the sugar content). Very hot drinks irritate the oesophagus and worsen nausea.

IzbegavajteAvoid

Alkohol tokom terapije. Ako je terapija dugotrajna, pitajte lekara.Alcohol during treatment. If treatment is long-term, ask your doctor.

Šta pojačava mučninuWhat makes nausea worse

Masna, pržena i veoma začinjena hrana; jaki mirisi (najčešće riblji); veoma slatka hrana; alkohol; mnogo kofeina; kod nekih citrusi; veoma vruća hrana i pića.Fatty, fried and heavily seasoned food; strong smells (fishy most often); very sweet food; alcohol; lots of caffeine; citrus for some people; very hot food and drinks.

Dok je imunitet slabWhile immunity is low

Šta izbegavati tokom terapijeWhat to avoid during treatment

  • Sirovo i nedovoljno pečeno meso, piletina i riba, uključujući suši i ostrigeRaw and undercooked meat, poultry and fish, including sushi and oysters
  • Hladno dimljena riba iz frižideraRefrigerated smoked fish
  • Sirova jaja i hrana sa njima: sirovo testo, domaći majonez, neki Cezar preliviRaw eggs and food made with them: raw cookie dough, homemade mayonnaise, some Caesar dressings
  • Nepasterizovano mleko, sokovi i sireviUnpasteurised milk, juices and cheeses
  • Prerađeno meso (viršle, salame, šunka, suvo meso): loše se podnosi i smatra se kancerogenimProcessed meat (hot dogs, deli meats, ham, jerky): poorly tolerated and considered carcinogenic
  • Sirove klice, neoprano voće i povrćeRaw sprouts, unwashed fruit and vegetables
  • Ostaci hrane koji stoje više dana u frižideruLeftovers that have sat in the fridge for days
  • Nepouzdana bunarska voda i zaslađena pićaUntested well water and sugary drinks
