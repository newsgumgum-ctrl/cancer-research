Za pacijente i porodiceFor patients and families

Znanje koje smirujeKnowledge that calms

Ovaj sajt je nastao iz pitanja jedne porodice koja se suočila sa olfaktornim neuroblastomom. Tražeći odgovore, slušali smo lekare i naučnike koji svakodnevno rade sa rakom. Ovde smo sve složili na jedno mesto, jednostavnim rečima.This site grew out of the questions of one family facing olfactory neuroblastoma. Looking for answers, we listened to doctors and scientists who work with cancer every day. Here we put it all in one place, in plain words.

BiologBiologist

Prof. Thomas Seyfried

Profesor biologije na Boston College-u. Nastavlja rad Ota Varburga i rak posmatra kao metaboličku bolest mitohondrija. Sam kaže da onkologija njegov pogled još ne prihvata.Professor of biology at Boston College. He continues Otto Warburg's work and sees cancer as a metabolic disease of the mitochondria. He says himself that oncology does not yet accept his view.

Tri stvari koje smo naučiliThree things we learned

Pitajte i zapisujteAsk and write it down

Svi lekari koje smo slušali ponavljaju isto: znajte svoj tip tumora, stadijum i plan. Na svakoj stranici su pitanja koja možete poneti na kontrolu.Every doctor we listened to says the same: know your tumour type, stage and plan. Each page has questions you can take to your appointment.

Ne upoređujte seDon't compare yourself

Dr Roman kaže da dva pacijenta na istoj terapiji mogu da se osećaju potpuno različito. Vaš put je samo vaš.Dr Roman says two patients on the same treatment can feel completely different. Your path is your own.

Ne odustajte od kontrolaKeep your check-ups

Šta god dodatno probali, snimanja i pregledi su jedini način da znate šta se zaista dešava.Whatever else you try, scans and check-ups are the only way to know what is really happening.
