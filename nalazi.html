Razumeti nalazeUnderstanding reports

Pročitajte svoj nalaz kao lekarRead your report like a doctor

Dr Roman

Dr Roman smatra da svaki pacijent treba da dobije kopiju svog nalaza i da ga razume. Ovde su njegova objašnjenja za pet tema. Otvorite onu koja vas zanima.Dr Roman believes every patient should get a copy of their report and understand it. Here are his explanations for five topics. Open the one you need.

Mamografija i BI-RADSMammograms and BI-RADS

Mamografija ne sprečava rak dojke, ali ga otkriva ranije, kada je lakše lečiv. To je jedina metoda za koju su randomizovane studije pokazale da smanjuje smrtnost od raka dojke; za ultrazvuk to ne važi. Ima malo zračenja, oko 0,4 mSv.A mammogram doesn't prevent breast cancer but finds it earlier, when it's more treatable. It is the only method shown in randomised trials to reduce breast cancer deaths; that isn't true for ultrasound. It carries little radiation, about 0.4 mSv.

KalcifikacijeCalcifications

Mamografija dobro vidi sitne naslage kalcijuma, prisutne kod oko 40% invazivnih karcinoma i oko 80% duktalnih karcinoma in situ. Ultrazvuk ih slabo vidi.Mammograms see tiny calcium deposits well; they appear in about 40% of invasive cancers and about 80% of ductal carcinoma in situ. Ultrasound sees them poorly.

Guste dojkeDense breasts

Mamografija promaši 20–30% karcinoma, najčešće kod gustih dojki (mlađe i vitkije žene). Radiolog tada predlaže dodatni pregled: ciljane snimke, MR, ultrazvuk ili mamografiju sa kontrastom.Mammograms miss 20–30% of cancers, most often in dense breasts (younger, leaner women). The radiologist then suggests an extra test: spot views, MRI, ultrasound or contrast mammography.

Saveti: pregledajte dojke same jednom mesečno (2–3 dana posle menstruacije), kod lekara jednom godišnje, i radite mamografije uvek na istom mestu da bi se snimci mogli porediti.Tips: examine your breasts monthly (2–3 days after your period), have a doctor's exam yearly, and get mammograms at the same place so images can be compared.

Rak prostate: rizik i lečenjeProstate cancer: risk and treatment

Kod većine muškaraca rak prostate nije uzrok smrti; „umiru sa njim, a ne od njega“. Zato je ključno proceniti rizik, da se ne leči ono što ne treba, ali i da se ne propusti agresivan tumor.For most men prostate cancer is not what kills them; they "die with it, not of it". So assessing risk is key: don't treat what doesn't need treating, but don't miss an aggressive tumour.

  1. TipType

    Adenokarcinom je 95–99% slučajeva. Retki tipovi (sitnoćelijski, neuroendokrini) leče se drugačije.Adenocarcinoma is 95–99% of cases. Rare types (small cell, neuroendocrine) are treated differently.

  2. ProširenostSpread

    Lokalni (samo prostata), regionalni (limfni čvorovi) ili metastatski. Najbolji snimak je PSMA PET.Local (prostate only), regional (lymph nodes) or metastatic. The best scan is a PSMA PET.

  3. Gleason i grupaGleason and grade group

    Gleason 6 = grupa 1; 7 (3+4) = grupa 2; 7 (4+3) = grupa 3; 8 = grupa 4; 9–10 = grupa 5. Viši broj, agresivniji tumor.Gleason 6 = group 1; 7 (3+4) = group 2; 7 (4+3) = group 3; 8 = group 4; 9–10 = group 5. Higher means more aggressive.

  4. PSAPSA

    Normalno ispod 4, ali niže kod mladih. Visok PSA nije uvek rak (npr. uvećana prostata), a 5–10% karcinoma uopšte ne stvara PSA.Normal is under 4, but lower in young men. A high PSA isn't always cancer (e.g. enlarged prostate), and 5–10% of cancers make no PSA at all.

Lokalni i regionalni rakLocal and regional cancer

  • Aktivno praćenje (PSA, snimanja) ili samo posmatranje kod očekivanog života kraćeg od 5 godinaActive surveillance (PSA, scans) or simple observation when life expectancy is under 5 years
  • Radikalna prostatektomija, obično uz uklanjanje limfnih čvorova karliceRadical prostatectomy, usually with pelvic lymph node removal
  • Zračenje spolja ili „semenke“ (brahiterapija)External radiation or "seed" implants (brachytherapy)
  • Fokalne metode (zamrzavanje, HIFU), sa manje podatakaFocal methods (freezing, HIFU), with less data

Hormonska i druge terapijeHormonal and other therapies

  • Snižavanje testosterona: agonisti LHRH (Lupron, Eligard), antagonisti (degareliks, relugoliks), uklanjanje testisaLowering testosterone: LHRH agonists (Lupron, Eligard), antagonists (degarelix, relugolix), removal of the testicles
  • Agonisti prvih 2–4 nedelje podignu testosteron („flare“), pa se kod velike bolesti daje i antiandrogen (npr. bikalutamid)Agonists raise testosterone for the first 2–4 weeks ("flare"), so with heavy disease an anti-androgen (e.g. bicalutamide) is added
  • Novi antiandrogeni: abirateron, enzalutamid, apalutamid, darolutamidNewer anti-androgens: abiraterone, enzalutamide, apalutamide, darolutamide
  • Kod metastaza: hemioterapija, genetsko testiranje (BRCA, HRR → PARP inhibitori), imunoterapija kod MSI-high ili TMB ≥10, radijum-223, lutecijum-177 PSMA, Provenge, lekovi za kostiWith metastases: chemotherapy, genetic testing (BRCA, HRR → PARP inhibitors), immunotherapy for MSI-high or TMB ≥10, radium-223, lutetium-177 PSMA, Provenge, bone drugs

Zanimljivo: lutecijum-177 se kod prostate vezuje za PSMA, a kod olfaktornog neuroblastoma za SSTR2. Isti princip „ciljanog zračenja iznutra“. Vidi stranicu o olfaktornom neuroblastomu.Interesting: in prostate cancer lutetium-177 targets PSMA, and in olfactory neuroblastoma it targets SSTR2. The same principle of "targeted radiation from inside". See the olfactory neuroblastoma page.

Rak jednjakaOesophageal cancer

Dva glavna tipa su skvamocelularni karcinom i adenokarcinom; leče se različito. Skvamocelularni je mnogo osetljiviji na zračenje. U nalazu biopsije potražite i diferencijaciju: dobro diferentovan raste sporije, slabo diferentovan agresivnije.The two main types are squamous cell carcinoma and adenocarcinoma; they are treated differently. Squamous cell is much more sensitive to radiation. In the biopsy report also look for differentiation: well differentiated grows slower, poorly differentiated more aggressively.

Lečenje po stadijumuTreatment by stage

  • Tis i T1a N0: endoskopsko uklanjanje, bez velike operacijeTis and T1a N0: endoscopic removal, no major surgery
  • T1b N0: najčešće ezofagektomija (uklanjanje dela jednjaka sa limfnim čvorovima)T1b N0: usually oesophagectomy (removing part of the oesophagus with lymph nodes)
  • T2 do T4a: terapija pre operacije, pa operacija, ili definitivna hemio-radioterapija bez operacijeT2 to T4a: treatment before surgery, then surgery, or definitive chemoradiation without surgery
  • T4b: definitivna hemio-radioterapijaT4b: definitive chemoradiation

Važno je pitatiWorth asking

  • Adenokarcinom: FLOT (hemio pre i posle operacije) je obično prvi izbor; studija ESOPEC je pokazala bolje preživljavanje nego CROSS. Oko polovine pacijenata ne završi FLOT posle operacije.Adenocarcinoma: FLOT (chemo before and after surgery) is usually preferred; the ESOPEC trial showed better survival than CROSS. About half of patients don't finish FLOT after surgery.
  • Skvamocelularni: posle hemio-radioterapije oko 50% nema živog tumora u operisanom tkivu; ako tumora ima, godinu dana imunoterapije (nivolumab).Squamous: after chemoradiation about 50% have no living tumour in the removed tissue; if tumour remains, a year of immunotherapy (nivolumab).
  • Da li je tumor MSI-high ili dMMR? Tada je imunoterapija veoma važna.Is the tumour MSI-high or dMMR? Then immunotherapy is very important.
  • Kod metastaza: PD-L1 (CPS) i mutacije NTRK, BRAF, RET.With metastases: PD-L1 (CPS) and NTRK, BRAF, RET mutations.

PlazmocitomPlasmacytoma

Plazmocitom je tumor od plazma ćelija, ćelija koje prave antitela. Većina pacijenata ima i multipli mijelom, a oko 5% ima samo jedan, „solitarni“ plazmocitom. U kosti je najčešće u kičmi. Van kosti se oko 80% javlja u glavi i vratu: u nosnoj šupljini, sinusima, ždrelu i grkljanu.A plasmacytoma is a tumour of plasma cells, the cells that make antibodies. Most patients also have multiple myeloma; about 5% have just one, "solitary" plasmacytoma. In bone it is most often in the spine. Outside bone about 80% occur in the head and neck: nasal cavity, sinuses, pharynx and larynx.

Šta se proveravaWhat gets checked

  • Krvna slika, biohemija (bubrezi, kalcijum, jetra), imunoglobulini, elektroforeza proteina seruma i urina, slobodni laki lanci, beta-2 mikroglobulinBlood count, chemistry (kidneys, calcium, liver), immunoglobulins, serum and urine protein electrophoresis, free light chains, beta-2 microglobulin
  • Biopsija koštane srži: nema klonalnih plazma ćelija, manje od 10%, ili 10% i višeBone marrow biopsy: no clonal plasma cells, under 10%, or 10% or more
  • MR celog tela ili PET-CTWhole-body MRI or PET-CT

Lečenje i praćenjeTreatment and follow-up

  • Solitarni plazmocitom: zračenje je prvi izbor; operacija u posebnim slučajevimaSolitary plasmacytoma: radiation first; surgery in special cases
  • Sa mijelomom: lečenje kao mijelom (sistemska terapija)With myeloma: treated as myeloma (systemic therapy)
  • Oko 50% pređe u mijelom za 5 godina posle zračenja; kod plazmocitoma kosti 35%, a van kosti 7% za 2 godineAbout 50% progress to myeloma within 5 years after radiation; for bone plasmacytoma 35%, outside bone 7% at 2 years
  • Analize na 3–6 meseci; snimanje jednom godišnje do 5 godina. Posle zračenja sačekajte nekoliko meseci pre prvog snimka, jer zračenje deluje još 3–5 meseci.Labs every 3–6 months; scans yearly for up to 5 years. After radiation wait a few months before the first scan, as radiation keeps working for 3–5 months.

Krvna slika i anemijaBlood counts and anaemia

Anemija je manjak hemoglobina: kod žena ispod 12, kod muškaraca ispod 13. Simptomi su umor, bledilo, glavobolja, opadanje kose, ubrzan rad srca, nemirne noge, a kod nedostatka gvožđa i neobična želja da se gricka led.Anaemia is low haemoglobin: under 12 in women, under 13 in men. Symptoms include fatigue, pallor, headache, hair loss, a racing heart, restless legs, and with iron deficiency an odd urge to chew ice.

TalasemijaThalassaemia

Visok RBC i veoma nizak MCV (60-ih), često porodična anemija. Ne treba davati gvožđe, jer je preopterećenje gvožđem glavna opasnost.High RBC with a very low MCV (in the 60s), often family anaemia. Iron should not be given, as iron overload is the main danger.

B12 i folna kiselinaB12 and folate

Veliki eritrociti (MCV često preko 110). Metilmalonska kiselina je povišena samo kod manjka B12. Posle operacije želuca B12 se obično daje u injekcijama.Large red cells (MCV often over 110). Methylmalonic acid is high only in B12 deficiency. After stomach surgery B12 is usually given by injection.

Anemija može imati više uzroka istovremeno, na primer manjak gvožđa i B12, što zbunjuje nalaze. Hronične bolesti i bubrezi takođe daju anemiju.Anaemia can have several causes at once, for example low iron and B12 together, which confuses results. Chronic illness and kidney disease also cause anaemia.
